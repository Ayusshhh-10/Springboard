General Rules

  • Read all instructions carefully before starting.
  • Ensure your camera is working correctly.
  • Keep your face clearly visible to the camera.
  • Remain in the examination environment during the exam.
  • Do not refresh or close the examination page unnecessarily.

Academic Integrity

  • Do not cheat or attempt to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Do not communicate with other candidates during the examination.
  • Do not use unauthorized external resources.
  • Do not attempt to bypass or disable the monitoring system.

AI Usage

  • Do not use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, or other AI assistants unless explicitly permitted by the examination administrator.
  • Do not open unauthorized AI tools or external websites during the examination.

Browser Activity

  • Avoid switching tabs or leaving the examination window.
  • Browser focus changes may be recorded as examination events.

Face Monitoring

  • Keep your face visible to the camera.
  • Avoid covering your face.
  • Avoid moving completely outside the camera frame.
  • Face-absent periods may contribute to monitoring events and the Integrity Score.

Identity

  • Identity verification is required before starting the examination.
  • The verified identity must correspond to the registered candidate.

Integrity Score

  • The examination session may receive an Integrity Score based on monitored session activity.
  • The Integrity Score is normalized between 0 and 100.
  • A higher score indicates fewer integrity concerns.
  • A lower score indicates more integrity concerns.

Risk Label

  • Low Risk: Integrity Score ≥ 80
  • Medium Risk: Integrity Score between 50 and 79
  • High Risk: Integrity Score < 50
